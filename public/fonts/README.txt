NEUE MONTREAL FONT PLACEHOLDER
==============================

The site is styled with "Neue Montreal" (Pangram Pangram — https://pangrampangram.com/products/neue-montreal),
a modern neo-grotesque / Arial-style sans.

How to activate it:
1. Get the trial files from Pangram Pangram (email signup, or buy the license)
2. Convert the .otf/.ttf files to .woff2 (e.g. https://cloudconvert.com or fonttools)
3. Drop the files here with EXACTLY these names:
   - NeueMontreal-Light.woff2     (weight 300)
   - NeueMontreal-Regular.woff2   (weight 400)
   - NeueMontreal-Medium.woff2    (weight 500)
   - NeueMontreal-Bold.woff2      (weight 700)
   - NeueMontreal-Black.woff2     (weight 900)

Until then the site renders with Inter (Google Fonts),
the closest free alternative to Neue Montreal.
